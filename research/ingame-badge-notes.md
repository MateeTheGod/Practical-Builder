# In-game badge notes from PBLab

Observations made in the live game while building and checking builds with PB Build Lab, November 2026. They complement the community threshold sheet used by the calculator. They are not developer-confirmed, and the game shows no version number.

## Threshold observations

Center, 7'0 / 7'2 / 275, None, Normal, Right (Player Upgrade screen, 2026-11-24). Driving Dunk was 25 and Vertical 71 in every reading.

| Standing Dunk | Strength | Traffic Finisher shown |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 87 | 92 | Diamond |
| 95 | 95 | Amethyst |
| 94 | 95 | Diamond |

With Driving Dunk at 25, the Dunk requirement for Traffic Finisher was met by Standing Dunk. Amethyst needed Standing Dunk 95 even with Strength 95, which matches this calculator's `95 Dunk & 94 Strength` rule with **Dunk = Standing Dunk** (or *Whichever is higher*). It contradicts reading the sheet's Strength path as Strength alone. Only Traffic Finisher was checked; the other Dunk badges are not established by these readings.

## Attribute link

On the same build, Moving Shot could not be lowered below **51** (cap 71) while Mid-Range Shot and Three-Point Shot were both 81. Raising Mid-Range or Three-Point raises Moving Shot, so lowering Moving Shot alone does not free points. On 2026-11-03 the default Small Forward showed the same kind of link: raising Three-Point Shot raised Mid-Range, and raising Mid-Range raised Three-Point and Moving Shot. The exact rule is not measured.

## Cost note

On that build the player could not afford Strength 92 → 95 until other ratings were lowered. This is consistent with the reported rule that each step from 90 upward costs 3, but it does not verify it.
